About Us

Lahli's Cakes was established in 2015 by Lahli Manyaka, a passionate baker with a dream to share her delicious creations with her community in Burgersfort, Limpopo.

What started as a small home-based business has grown into a beloved local bakery known for its quality ingredients, attention to detail, and exceptional customer service.

We believe that every celebration deserves a special cake, and we take pride in creating custom cakes that make your events memorable. All our cakes are baked fresh to order using the finest ingredients and traditional baking methods.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, wedding, anniversary, or just want a sweet treat, we're here to make it special with our delicious cakes.
